VERSION_CHANGES.txt

A short log of what changed in RighteousHack, newest first.
Each entry is a date and a sentence or two, enough to know that
something changed. The full customization inventory is
RIGHTEOUSHACK_CHANGES.md.

Planned (not done)
RighteousHack will eventually replace lawful, neutral, and chaotic
with two alignments, good and evil. Alignment still works as it
does now until that change is made.

2026-11-01
The Philistine Champion uses the ogre lord's attacks, speed, and
other combat numbers. He is always hostile and wields a
two-handed sword. The name is still Philistine Champion.

2026-11-01
The Philistine Champion waits in his own dark room on Mines' End,
far from the stairs. A winding corridor reaches that room without
crossing the letter floor, so the scholar's scroll can be taken
before the puzzle. He is still the only monster there.

2026-11-01
Mines' End is guarded by the Philistine Champion, the only monster
there. He is a difficulty-10 warrior with a long sword, scale mail,
a helmet, and a small shield, and he does not use sorcery. Killing
him leaves a scholar's scroll. Reading it shows the Greek name
ΠΕΤΡΑ and does not use the scroll up. It cannot be wished for.

2026-11-01
The Shield of Faith in the Babylon's Palace tower chest is
blessed and +2. The oil that shares that chest is unchanged.

2026-11-01
The old wizard mode is called prophet mode. Help, the guidebook,
and the messages that name the mode say prophet mode. The
playmode option accepts prophet. The old value debug still works.
The Wizard of Yendor and the Wizard role are unchanged.

2026-11-01
The dungeon overview tags the Prophet's level [prophet] and
Capernaum [capernaum]. The feature line is "Prophet of the Lord."
The #levelport menu uses those same names. The xlogfile records
consulted_the_prophet and entered_capernaum. The cats in
Capernaum are housecats, so the town loads.

2026-11-01
The Oracle is the Prophet. A minor word is one of the placeholder
proverbs. The major word is "The treasure you seek has always
been yours." The big room is the Valley of Dry Bones. The castle
is Babylon's Palace and its prize is the Shield of Faith. The
bridge opens with the Key to Babylon's Palace, not a tune.

2026-11-01
Minetown is Capernaum, a lit fishing town. Peter the Fisherman
stands in front of a closet. Mines' End is the PETRA letter
puzzle. Bring Peter's net back to him. He keeps it, speaks his
thank-you, and gives you both keys. The closet holds a +2
Breastplate of Righteousness.

2026-11-01
Favor is the word you see for luck. The gray stone from the mines
is a Favor Stone. It works the same way. The Gnomish Mines are
the Salt Mines of Galilee.

2026-11-01
When the Lord anoints you, the words are "I anoint thee...  The
Servant of the Lord!" The attributes screen says "the Servant of
the Lord" and the livelog says you were anointed. The gift that used
to come with that moment is a placeholder and is not given yet.

2026-11-01
Crystal balls are gone, except the Champion's Orb of Fate.
Fortune cookies are gone. In their place are four placeholder
Prophet proverbs, exact King James wording, for Jon to review.
Sacrificing your own kind does not summon a demon or change the
altar. Random Moloch temples and priests are not generated.

2026-11-01
King Solomon tells you what he asked for, and quotes Mark 8:36,
before you pray at the Altar at Gibeon. Both lines are spoken
on #chat until the vision has happened.

2026-11-01
At the Altar at Gibeon the LORD speaks the King James text of
1 Kings 3:5 and 1 Kings 3:11-13.

2026-11-01
King Solomon also quotes Mark 8:36 when you #chat. He still
says his line about asking for a heart to judge rightly.

2026-11-01
The boots artifact is named Gospel of Peace. The old spelling
Gosple of the Peace is gone.

2026-11-01
The Oracle's chamber has an up stair to the Cave of Gibeon. Praying
on the Altar at Gibeon with the burnt offering there asks for one
wish. Asking for wisdom gives the existing Belt of Truth at +2,
a blessed anointing oil of gain level, and Wisdom 25.

2026-11-01
An adult dragon steak weighs 4500, past what stock pickup can lift
(the carrying cap is 1000, and the heaviest single lift is just
under 3000). It stays on the floor, where it can be cooked and
eaten. A baby dragon steak weighs 1500 and can be carried.

2026-11-01
The food workbook shows monster weights as numbers. A dragon
weighs 4500, a baby dragon 1500, a human 1450, and a jelly 50.

2026-11-01
Goblins leave raw goblin meat and cooked goblin meat, with the
normal raw-meat rules. Hobgoblins and the other orcs still leave
nothing.

2026-11-01
Fish fillets still skip the raw-poisoning chance, and they spoil
like other raw meat. A raw fillet cannot be blessed. Cooking slows
that spoil, and blessed cooked fillet does not spoil. Lizard meat
and jellies still do not spoil.

2026-11-01
Liches crumble into dust and leave nothing to eat. Cyclops meat
is named "raw Cyclops steak" and "cooked Cyclops steak".

2026-11-01
Demons leave an essence, and eating any demon's essence is fatal.
Mind flayers, liches, nymphs, the goblin, and a few named people
leave an essence too. Apes, were-creatures, and Cyclops leave meat.
Jellies, lizard meat, and fish fillets do not spoil. A jelly's
name is no longer said twice.

2026-10-31
Spirits leave an essence, named like "wraith's essence" or
"Death's essence". It never spoils, cannot be cooked, and can be
blessed. Ghosts, shades, wraiths, barrow wights, Nazgul, vampires,
zombies, mummies, ghouls, skeletons, and the three Riders do this.
A wraith's essence still raises your level. Eating a Rider's
essence is still fatal, and they still come back from it. Trolls
still leave meat. Cannibalism is gone: no penalty for eating your
own kind, and no cave dweller or orc exception.

2026-10-31
Monster bodies are raw meat now (a dragon leaves a raw dragon steak).
Human-like monsters leave none, except trolls, who still do so
they can come back. Eating raw meat can fatally poison
you. #cook, with a flint and tinder box, makes cooked meat that
spoils much more slowly. Blessed cooked meat does not spoil. The
full list is in docs/RighteousHack_Food.xlsx. Saves from before
this change will not load.

2026-10-31
The attributes screen no longer says your god is opposed by the
other names in the pantheon. It only says which god you are on a
mission for. Planned, not done: those three alignments will
eventually become good and evil.

2026-10-31
Magic cancellation now says you are protected from false prophets,
with a degree when the armor gives one. Half spell damage now says
you take half damage from false prophecies. A monster's magic is
called sorcery.

2026-10-31
The new NetHack 5.0 wand was added as the Rod of Stillness
(Psalm 46:10). Zapping it still makes the current level block
teleportation for a short time.

2026-10-31
Windows builds: build.bat now refreshes src\Makefile from
sys\windows\Makefile.nmake before compiling, so an old 3.7
makefile is not reused.

2026-10-31
Upgraded the game from its NetHack 3.7 base to NetHack 5.0.0.
RighteousHack's own names, items, roles, and quests were kept.
Save files from the 3.7 version will not load.

2026-07-19
RighteousHack v0.1, the version this upgrade started from: a
Christian retelling of NetHack 3.7, with renamed gear, fewer
roles, new quest artifacts, and a browser map editor.
