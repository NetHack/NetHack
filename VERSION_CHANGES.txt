VERSION_CHANGES.txt

A short log of what changed in RighteousHack, newest first.
Each entry is a date and a sentence or two, enough to know that
something changed. The full customization inventory is
RIGHTEOUSHACK_CHANGES.md.

Planned (not done)
RighteousHack will eventually replace lawful, neutral, and chaotic
with two alignments, good and evil. Alignment still works as it
does now until that change is made.

2026-11-03
The Sanctum level is called the Sanctum of the Great Dragon.
A smite from its unaligned shrine names the Great Dragon, not
Moloch. The bolt and the odds are unchanged.

2026-11-03
Entering the Sanctum temple, the high priest says "Welcome, we
have long awaited thee. Look thou within the chest, and receive
thy reward." After the yes path, an attack that would smite you
for striking that priest is turned aside, and the priest is
consumed. Before that, the smite is unchanged.

2026-11-03
Opening the chest that holds your soul shows four paragraphs on
one black screen, the same kind of screen as a Wilderness floor
intro. The last is Romans 10:9, with no citation added. After
that screen, the only question is "Dost thou confess with thy
mouth the Lord Jesus, and believe in thine heart that God hath
raised him from the dead?" Yes and no are the only answers.

2026-11-03
Every melee hit with the Sword of the Spirit adds dice equal to
your experience level, each die having 2 sides, on top of the
blade and the holy light. A holy-light hit that does not paralyze
still shows the ordinary hit line.

2026-11-03
The first time the Greed gate sends you back, it says Matthew
19:22: "But when the young man heard that saying, he went away
sorrowful: for he had great possessions." Every bounce after
that says "Thou must leave all behind to pass this way."

2026-11-03
Each Fear map still places 40 monsters. The master lich, the
arch-lich, and the titan are now a minotaur, an ettin, and a
storm giant, five of each. Blue dragon, jabberwock, purple worm,
pit fiend, and balrog are unchanged.

2026-11-03
Royal Historian, Healer, Priest, and Prophet can train long
sword to Basic. Knight, Adventurer, and Champion are unchanged.

2026-11-03
The Sword of the Spirit is blessed, +2, and erodeproof, with no
rust or burn on it. That one flag stops rust, fire, corrosion,
and rot. Once identified, the iron blade shows as rustproof.

2026-11-03
The #enhance screen heads the spell schools "Prophetic Skills".
The seven names are spiritual warfare, healing, discernment,
blessing, prayer, deliverance, and creation.

2026-11-03
Each Wilderness floor shows a full-screen intro once, the
first time you arrive. The desert says he or she was led into
the wilderness. Greed, Pride, and Fear add Luke 12:15,
Proverbs 16:18, and 2 Timothy 1:7 under the verse. The garden
has no reference. On the Fear path or either stair, monsters
still walk up and crowd the tile, but they do not start an
attack. Stepping off, they attack at full strength. A Psalm 91
you engrave yourself is unchanged. The Cave of Wonders clock
starts at 200 turns. Every accepted prayer adds 50 and says
"The Lord is pleased with you and has restored your strength."
Picking up a gold pile or a gem costs 25, and the command
then says that greed has hastened your hunger. At 30 or fewer the
countdown shows, and it stops if a prayer lifts you above 30.
At 0 the tombstone is unchanged. A Pride reflection has 60 hit
points. Its level and its drain both stop at 4. Finger of
execution casts at a flat 50 percent and kills through resistance
and reflection. It leaves the spell list when you leave Pride.
The Rod of Frozen Power burst is blue and stays on screen about
a second and a half longer. The Garden altar under the Sword of
the Spirit has no engraving. The sword is a blessed +2. Praying
on that altar crowns you, the Servant of the Lord, if you are
not already. The Pride altar prayer says one line. With a lure
in the pack, even nested in a bag: "You drop to your knees and
repent of your pride. The treasures crumble into dust, and a
hidden way opens." With no lure: "The treasures crumble into
dust, and a hidden way opens." Each Fear map placed 8 monsters
and now places 40, five of each of the same eight, all off the
Psalm 91 path and the stairs. A wish that names a Pride lure is
refused the way a quest artifact is: "For a moment, you feel
something in your hands, but it disappears!" The plain item,
without that name, can still be wished for.

2026-11-01
The quest object is your own soul, in a chest on the Sanctum
altar. It is named for you, such as "Elisha's soul". Opening
that chest is the reveal and God's question. Either answer, the
hosts of darkness rise. Yes: they move for two turns and cannot
fight, then the light freezes the whole level and you receive a
+2 Cap of Salvation. The code name is still Cap of Salvation.
The climb back up is only a placeholder, the Road to Salvation.
No: they really attack for three turns, then the game ends.
The tombstone is "Killed by the hosts of darkness, having
refused redemption." The closing line is God's spoken line, not
a quoted verse. The high priest no longer carries the Amulet.

2026-10-31
Spirits leave an essence, named like "wraith's essence" or
"Death's essence". It never spoils, cannot be cooked, and can be
blessed. Ghosts, shades, wraiths, barrow wights, Nazgul, vampires,
zombies, mummies, ghouls, skeletons, and the three Riders do this.
A wraith's essence still raises your level. Eating a Rider's
essence is still fatal, and they still come back from it. Trolls
still leave meat. Cannibalism is gone: no penalty for eating your
own kind, and no cave dweller or orc exception.

2026-10-31
Monster bodies are raw meat now (a dragon leaves a raw dragon steak).
Human-like monsters leave none, except trolls, who still do so
they can come back. Eating raw meat can fatally poison
you. #cook, with a flint and tinder box, makes cooked meat that
spoils much more slowly. Blessed cooked meat does not spoil. The
full list is in docs/RighteousHack_Food.xlsx. Saves from before
this change will not load.
